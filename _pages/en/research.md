# Academic interests {#research}

My academic interests include competitive programming, algorithms, data structures, and computing education, including critical evaluation of responses produced by generative AI.

## Competitive programming

- Analysis of problems and contests from the Brazilian Informatics Olympiad (OBI) and the Maratona SBC de Programação
- Using programming contest problems to teach and learn algorithms
- Preparing teams for competitions

## Algorithms and data structures

- Algorithm design and analysis
- Time and space complexity
- Comparing solutions and choosing suitable approaches

## Computing education

- Teaching programming in technical and vocational education
- Developing problems and assessments
- Quality and limitations of generative AI responses
