# Academic interests {#research}

My academic interests include competitive programming, algorithms, data structures, and computing education, including critical evaluation of responses produced by generative AI.

## Undergraduate thesis (2023)

**Uma análise das provas da Primeira Fase da Maratona SBC de Programação de 2013 a 2023.** Undergraduate thesis in Computer Science at UPF, supervised by Marcelo Trindade Rebonatto.

The study examined first-round Maratona SBC de Programação results from 2013 to 2023, classified problems by difficulty using teams' contest outcomes, and mapped problem topics to the Brazilian Computer Society's reference curriculum. It used third-party data-extraction scripts, with adaptations acknowledged in the thesis; the analyses and classifications are described in its methodology. This is an **undergraduate thesis**, not a journal publication.

## Undergraduate research (2013–2014)

**PROBIC/Fapergs scholarship holder at UPF**, from December 2013 to February 2014, 20 hours per week, on the project *Tecnologias e metodologias de inclusão digital na cibercultura: por uma cultura hacker na educação?*, supervised by Adriano Canabarro Teixeira. The role and period are documented by an institutional certificate.
